# Firefly default lesson content-completeness gate

This bounded slice deepens the already-merged Firefly default lesson without changing its 10-card structure, boss experiment, one-gold state machine, learner-state authority, or search/research runtime.

Acceptance for the expanded cards:

- Card 1 separates official character facts from gameplay interpretation and explains why the Complete Combustion window creates action pressure.
- Card 2 explains the charge phase, burst phase, action choices, and survival loop rather than only listing buttons.
- Card 3 explains how toughness damage, weakness break efficiency, break effect, defense, resistance, and break vulnerability relate, including pre-break versus post-break behavior.
- Card 4 explains each Firefly eidolon/light-cone choice as mechanism -> affected resource -> environment-dependent value.
- Cards 5-7 explain Dahlia, Fugue, Lingsha, and Gallagher as changes to the team damage/rotation structure, not just skill summaries.
- Card 8 states what each team upgrade gains and what remains missing or is traded away.
- Card 9 keeps six separate causal tracks: toughness damage, Super Break multiplier, defense, resistance, break vulnerability, and rotation resources.
- Card 10 explains why enemy mechanics change investment value, including cases where a normally strong investment loses value.

The default collapsed cards remain compact. Only the expanded layer becomes denser.

## Frozen data-depth scope

Five core characters are complete-data scope:

- Firefly
- The Dahlia
- Fugue
- Lingsha
- Gallagher

For each of those five, the same page must expose:

- practical max-rank Basic/Skill/Ultimate/Talent values and any enhanced forms used by the kit;
- the Technique as a first-class row rather than an omitted footnote;
- Energy, toughness damage using the lesson's 10/20/30 convention, durations, trigger caps, and resource costs;
- all three major traces / extra abilities;
- exact E1-E6 effects;
- exact values for the core Light Cones that this lesson actually recommends or compares, with the displayed superimposition level made explicit.

Firefly must use the post-4.2 kit and must not mix legacy pre-4.2 values into visible copy.

The complete numeric archive is separate from the analytical cards so that the lesson can stay readable without hiding exact data. Basic Attack defaults to Lv.6; Skill/Ultimate/Talent default to Lv.10; E3/E5 rank-cap changes remain visible through the eidolon text. Full Lv.1-15 growth tables are not dumped into the default expanded view. The Light Cone section is intentionally limited to options used by the lesson; it is not a full game-wide Light Cone database.

Harmony Trailblazer, Asta, and Ruan Mei are route-support scope only unless they later receive their own complete-data surface. The route card may show the exact values needed for the current team comparison, but it must not imply that their full skills, traces, eidolons, or light-cone data are covered.
